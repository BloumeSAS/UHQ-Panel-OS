# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want UHQ Panel OS to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Assume good intent and give helpful, actionable feedback.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory or sexualised language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Using the project to plan or promote illegal activity.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not follow this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **contact@bloume.fr**. All reports are reviewed confidentially.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
